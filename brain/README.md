# Brilliantaire Brain

Open this folder as an Obsidian vault when you want the private brain layer.

This folder is intentionally plain Markdown. It can be edited in Obsidian, VSCode, Codex, Antigravity, or any other local tool without lock-in.

## Folder Map

- `00-command-center` - weekly direction and current priorities.
- `01-brilliantaire-core` - identity, doctrine, principles, and positioning.
- `02-labs` - AI, software, automation, tools, and prototypes.
- `03-media` - visual storytelling, videos, campaigns, and content systems.
- `04-strategy` - brand systems, rollout frameworks, offers, and consulting.
- `05-tree-groove-records` - music, artists, releases, performance, and campaigns.
- `06-academy` - lessons, templates, guides, workshops, and education.
- `07-ventures` - products, platforms, partnerships, revenue, and scale.
- `08-idea-vault` - raw ideas before they become projects.
- `09-weekly-reviews` - reviews, metrics, lessons, and next moves.

## Operating Rule

Capture raw thoughts quickly, then route them into the right lane.

Do not let good ideas stay only in chat. Convert them into notes, projects, assets, or scheduled work.
