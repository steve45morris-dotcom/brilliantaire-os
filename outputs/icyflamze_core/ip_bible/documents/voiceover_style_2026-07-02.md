# Voiceover Style Guide: ICYFLAMZE CORE

- **Style Tone:** Poetic, strategic, street-smart, philosophical, scientific, calm but powerful, founder-minded, legacy-driven.

---

## 🔒 Voiceover Style Rules
- Speak with calculated cadence. Pause on terminal transitions.
- Maintain a calm, strategic tone. Avoid hype voice styles.
- Frame street knowledge as a scientific study.

## 🗣️ Sentence Style
- Rhythmic, declarative, short structures.
- Uses contrasts (e.g. "They built... I ran...") and mathematical/chess motifs.

## 📚 Vocabulary Bank
- **Allowed words:** grid, gridlines, board, position, strike, spark, calculated, pressure, formula, legacy, mainframe, sovereign, checkmate, lighter, carbon, system.
- **Banned phrases:** industry standard, generic path, viral trend, normal routine, hype track, magic formula.

## 📝 Narration Examples
- Example 1: "They built a stage. I built a grid. They chase the signal; I write the algorithm."
- Example 2: "Flick the lighter. The gold sparks first, but it's the blue that holds the heat. That's the science of sound."
