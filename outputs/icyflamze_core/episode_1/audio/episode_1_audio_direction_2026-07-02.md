# Audio & Voiceover Direction: Episode 1 Trailer

This document outlines soundscapes and voice generation settings.

---

## 🎙️ Voice Profile
- **Voice Type:** Calm, resonant, low-register male baritone with deep strategic timbre.
- **Delivery Tone:** Poetic, street-smart, philosophical, calm but powerful, founder-minded.
- **Pacing:** Measured, deliberate. Approximately 110 words per minute. Notable pauses on sound effect marks.

## 🎼 Soundtrack & Music Bed
- **Bed Direction:** Cerebral synth pads, low monospace hum, followed by a heavy, slow boom-bap rhythm (80 BPM) with bass-boosted sub drops.

## 🎛️ Sound Design Cues
- **Risers:** Digital filter sweeps rising slowly from 0:10 to 0:18.
- **Impact Hits:** Deep sub-bass drop at 0:16 (corresponds to 'decoded'). Heavy metallic thuds at chess piece cues.
- **Title Reveal Sound:** An analog synthesizer chord with digital terminal heartbeat loop.
- **Outro Sound:** Fading boom-bap percussion layers down to a simple monospace keyboard click sound.

## 🛠️ Speech Synthesis Parameters
- **Piper Voice Notes:** Configure voice engine using standard model 'en_US-danny-low' at speed 0.95 for optimal baritone depth.
- **ElevenLabs Settings:** Use 'Marcus' or custom strategic voice model with stability set to 80% and clarity set to 90% to avoid theatrical swings.
