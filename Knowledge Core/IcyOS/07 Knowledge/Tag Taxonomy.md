`Version: 1.0.0` | `Status: Active`

This document details the metadata formats, tags, and properties to keep the Obsidian vault query-friendly.

---

## 🏷️ Standard Tags
- `#daily-log`: Operational logs.
- `#meeting-sync`: Minutes and action cards.
- `#architecture-decision` or `#adr`: ADR files.
- `#technology-research`: Technology research briefs.
- `#sprint-planning`: Active and completed sprints.
- `#project-matrix`: Subsystem specs.
