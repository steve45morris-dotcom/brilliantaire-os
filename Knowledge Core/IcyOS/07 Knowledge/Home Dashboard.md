## 🏷️ Tag Taxonomy Guide
To keep Dataview working smoothly, tag notes with these standard taxonomies:
- `#daily-log`: User and system log tracker files.
- `#meeting-sync`: Minutes and action cards from meetings.
- `#architecture-decision` or `#adr`: System design records.
- `#technology-research`: Technology research records.
- `#sprint-planning`: Active and completed sprint pages.
- `#project-matrix`: Subsystem specs and projects.
