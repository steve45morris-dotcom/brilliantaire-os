## 🚀 The North Star Metric
> **Continuously transform human intention into meaningful execution with the least possible cognitive effort.**

---

## 📈 Key Indicators (KPIs)
1. **Friction Minimization**: Minutes spent from raw intention capture to approved mission timeline (< 3 minutes).
2. **Execution Integrity**: Ratio of successful, verified agent runs to total code proposals (> 95%).
3. **Context Ingestion Rate**: Time required for a newly booted AI agent to parse spec files and resume work (< 15 seconds).
