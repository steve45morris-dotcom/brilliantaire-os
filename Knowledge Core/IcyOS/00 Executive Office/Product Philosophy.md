# 💡 ICOS Product Philosophy
`Version: 1.0.0` | `Status: Active`

## 🧩 Core Beliefs
- **AI Plans, Human Approves**: AI manages detail layout, scheduling constraints, database models, and workspace validations. The strategist reviews the plan, edits timeline durations, and grants execution permission.
- **Cinematic Visual Minimalism**: High contrast, rich aesthetics, glassmorphism card panels, and smooth transitions are built directly into the UI layers to reduce cognitive friction.
- **Local Sovereignty**: User context, calendars, and files live locally. Remote sync triggers are gated and secure.
