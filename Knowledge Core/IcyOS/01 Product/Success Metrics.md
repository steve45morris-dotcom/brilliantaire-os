## 📈 Target KPIs

2. **Context Recovery Time**: Time required for a newly initialized AI agent to start tasks (< 15 seconds).
3. **Protected Buffer Preservation**: Percentage of sleep and rest calendar buffers kept free of task intrusions (> 98%).
