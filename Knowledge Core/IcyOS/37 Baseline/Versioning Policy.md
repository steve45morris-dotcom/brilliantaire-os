## 📋 Semantic Version Rules
- **Major (1.x.x)**: Breaking architectural shifts.
- **Minor (x.1.x)**: New verified capabilities or refactors.
- **Patch (x.x.1)**: Minor bug checks or specs clarifications.

*I build before burning.*
