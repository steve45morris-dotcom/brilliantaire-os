# Design Review Rubric

Score 0-10 each: brand distinction, typography, layout hierarchy, spacing consistency, color discipline, imagery and visual interest, responsive behavior, interaction quality, accessibility, conversion clarity. Cite a screenshot, DESIGN.md rule, DOM/state check, or test for every score. Thresholds: internal 80, business landing 85, flagship public 90. Missing desktop, mobile, independent review, or unresolved blockers forces `revise` regardless of total.
