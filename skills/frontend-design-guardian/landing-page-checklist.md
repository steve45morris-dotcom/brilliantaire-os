# Landing Page Checklist

- Product or offer is unmistakable in the first viewport.
- The primary action is specific, visible, and repeated only where useful.
- Hero uses authentic product/person/place imagery when inspection matters.
- Content order answers value, proof, objections, and action.
- Sections vary according to content rather than repeating one card grid.
- Claims and testimonials are verified.
- Mobile retains a clear first action and a hint of following content.
